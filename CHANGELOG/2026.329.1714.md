`nvidia` box (base `fedora-nonfree`) that **does not exist**. The Fedora GPU box was `nvidia-cdi`,

on the `podcdi.pod-cdi` base.

with the layer properties and its real consumers. The **CDI host layer** section (containerd
handler, durable refresh units, rootless spec link) is unchanged — it is still correct.
